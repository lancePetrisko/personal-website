Best specs:
  - Resolution: 1200×900px (4:3, retina-sharp on card size, not bloated)
  - Format: JPEG for photos (quality ~80), PNG only if need transparency
  - File size: aim under 300KB, compress with squoosh/tinypng before
    commit
  - Crop: subject centered — card crops edges on non-4:3 originals. Wide
    screenshot? use .exp-img-card--left class, crops from left edge
    instead of center.
