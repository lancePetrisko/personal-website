Overview

3d-printing blender lidar

This page is the expanded overview of my 3D4E campus model project. The goal was to create an accurate, physical topographic model of the University of Utah campus for showcase presentation.

Project Focus: terrain generation, model cleanup, large scale printing, and presentation at a regional event.

02

How It Was Built

workflow modix-120x fdm
  • Imported LiDAR data into Blender for terrain generation
  • Integrated official campus building models
  • Scaled and aligned structures for accurate placement
  • Prepared print ready geometry in slicing software
  • Produced the final model on a Modix 120X printer
03

Showcase + Impact

presentation ucla usc

Me and Nathan Melish represented the "Model the U Team" for the University of Utah's 3D Printing for Everyone (3D4E) Club and presented the final campus model alongside other member chapters from UCLA and USC.
